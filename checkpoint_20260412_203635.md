## Key observations

### What worked
1. **Multi-token mask fix** (fix #1): inserting N `[MASK]` tokens per entity instead of 1 was the most impactful single change — though the 67% → 17% gap was revealed to be memorization, not generalization.
2. **Proper train/test split**: exposed that the model was memorizing, not generalizing. True accuracy is ~17-20%, not 67%.
3. **bert-base-uncased over DistilBERT**: +3.18 pp on held-out set (20.52% vs 17.34%). BERT's extra capacity is being used — loss curve steeper, drops further.
4. **Janitor filtering**: 32 non-article pages (Category:, Template:, redirects) were silently polluting the corpus. Test suite caught this.

### What didn't work
- **More epochs alone** (3→7 on DistilBERT): +0.29 pp, within noise.
- **Error-driven weighted redaction**: marginal at best on this corpus size (18.21% vs 17.34%).

### What we suspect
- The corpus is too small (90 training articles) to generalize well. BERT is likely overfitting even with 7 epochs.
- DATE and PERSON remain weak (6.85% and 10.71%) — these are high-frequency but high-variety labels; the model hasn't seen enough examples.
- Labels with small test counts (MONEY=1, TIME=1, LAW=2) are not meaningful to track until corpus grows.
